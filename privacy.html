Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Portfolio AI Copilot collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services.

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Who we are

Portfolio AI Copilot is an early-stage product currently offered as a private beta. References in this Privacy Policy to “Portfolio AI Copilot”, “we”, “us” or “our” refer to the individual or entity responsible for operating the Portfolio AI Copilot application and related services.

The product name and business structure may change as the product evolves. If a material change affects how personal information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, Portfolio AI Copilot may process the following categories of information.

Account information

  • Name or profile information you provide.
  • Email address.
  • Account identifiers and authentication information required to create and maintain your account.

Portfolio and investment-related information

  • Holdings and portfolio positions.
  • Investment-related information you choose to enter, connect or make available to the application.
  • Portfolio allocation, transaction-related records, performance information and other information required to provide portfolio analysis and related functionality.

The exact information processed depends on the features available in the version of the application you use and the information you choose to provide or connect.

App and service information

We may process limited technical and operational information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the application. This may include device or application information, server logs, error information and records of interactions with the service.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the application may use the information required to deliver service-related notifications, alerts or updates to your device. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

3. How we use information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to operate and improve Portfolio AI Copilot and provide the features you request.

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect account security.
  • Display and analyse portfolio and investment-related information.
  • Generate portfolio context, insights, opportunities, recommendations and other product functionality.
  • Monitor system performance, investigate errors and improve reliability.
  • Communicate important service-related information.
  • Detect, prevent and investigate misuse, fraud or security issues.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use personal or portfolio information for the purpose of selling advertising based on that information.

4. AI-generated insights

Portfolio AI Copilot uses software, data processing and analytical methods to help organise information and surface portfolio context, market intelligence, opportunities and other insights.

AI-generated or automated outputs may be incomplete, inaccurate, delayed or unsuitable for your individual circumstances. Outputs are intended to support your own research and decision-making and should not be treated as a guarantee of performance or outcome.

Certain versions or features of Portfolio AI Copilot may allow you to connect information from a broker or another third-party service.

Where such a connection is available and you choose to use it, we may process the information made available through that connection to provide the requested functionality, such as displaying holdings, portfolio context or analysis.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

You should review the relevant third-party terms and privacy information before connecting an account or service.

6. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal or portfolio information. We may, however, disclose or process information in limited circumstances necessary to operate the service.

  • With infrastructure, hosting, authentication, notification or other service providers that help us operate Portfolio AI Copilot on our behalf.
  • With third-party services that you choose to connect, where necessary to provide the feature you request.
  • When required to comply with applicable law, legal process or a valid governmental request.
  • To protect the rights, security, integrity or safety of Portfolio AI Copilot, its users or others.
  • In connection with a legitimate business transfer, restructuring, acquisition or similar transaction, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

7. Data storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no application, network or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should protect your account credentials and notify us if you believe your account has been accessed without authorisation.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain your account, meet legal or security requirements, resolve disputes and enforce applicable agreements.

Because Portfolio AI Copilot is currently in beta, data handling and retention practices may evolve as the product develops. Material changes to our data practices will be reflected in an updated Privacy Policy.
