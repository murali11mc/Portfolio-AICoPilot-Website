Why we’re building it

Professional funds have teams, systems and layers of research. Individual investors often have a broker app and a stream of disconnected information. We believe AI can narrow that gap — with transparency and user control at the centre.

MISSION

Make sophisticated portfolio intelligence more accessible.

Give individual investors a deeper understanding of their portfolio, the market around it and the decisions in front of them.

VISION

An AI Copilot that works alongside the investor — not instead of them.

We want investing assistance to be intelligent, explainable and firmly under the user’s control.
